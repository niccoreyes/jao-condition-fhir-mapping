### Mortality rationale and qualification

The **current published** [VRDR v3.0.0 Cause of Death Part 1](https://hl7.org/fhir/us/vrdr/StructureDefinition-vrdr-cause-of-death-part1.html) is an Observation for a certifier-asserted line of the causal chain. It uses LOINC **69453-9** for the US Part I cause line, `Observation.valueCodeableConcept.text` for original wording, and a line-number component. [MDI v2.0.0 Cause of Death Part 1](https://hl7.org/fhir/us/mdi/StructureDefinition-Observation-mdi-cause-of-death-part1.html) derives from that VRDR Observation and requires a certifier (`performer`) or a data-absent reason. The distinct VRDR [manual](https://hl7.org/fhir/us/vrdr/StructureDefinition-vrdr-manual-underlying-cause-of-death.html) and [automated](https://hl7.org/fhir/us/vrdr/StructureDefinition-vrdr-automated-underlying-cause-of-death.html) underlying-cause Observations use LOINC **80359-3** and **80358-5** respectively and a coded result. Those are **not** interchangeable with a certifier-authored Part I line, and neither is a generic mortality/deceased-status indicator. Row 9 names an ICD-10 underlying cause but does not say whether it is the certified line or a post-certification manual/automated code. Row 10 might be verbatim wording or a code display. Confirm stage, line number, certifier, matching assertion, and national ICD system/version before fixing `Observation.code`, populating `value.coding`, or publishing a PH profile. Do not silently import the US certificate LOINC or its US value set.

### Terminology opportunities (review candidates, not bindings)

- [SNOMED CT **840539006**](https://docs.snomed.org/implementation-guides/snomed-ct-guide-for-covid-19/3-mapping-to-icd-10) denotes COVID-19 disease. It can qualify a **confirmed** positive Condition, but not row 7's Yes/No case-status question or a negative answer.
- **398254007** denotes pre-eclampsia, which is narrower than all pregnancy-induced hypertension; use only for confirmed pre-eclampsia, not row 29's question. **154283005** denotes [pulmonary tuberculosis](https://evsexplore.semantics.cancer.gov/evsexplore/concept/snomedct_us/154283005), not row 48's answer or a negative result.
- [LOINC **69453-9**](https://loinc.org/69453-9) is the US certificate Part I line, not an unconditional Philippine underlying-cause question. [**80359-3**](https://loinc.org/80359-3) is a *manually assigned* underlying ICD code and [**80358-5**](https://loinc.org/80358-5) an *automatically selected* one. Choose only with workflow evidence.
- ICD-10 I10–I15, I60–I64, E10–E14 and A15–A16 are ranges, not patient codes. GOLD grade/group need versioned scales. For unspecific diagnoses, symptoms, history, PWD classifications and answers, do not insert a superficially plausible SNOMED/LOINC code just to increase coding density. Prefer exact source meaning and approved PH value sets; retain verbatim text where no precise code exists.
