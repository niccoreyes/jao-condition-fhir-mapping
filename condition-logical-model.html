One resource is not one meaning

The sheet mixes longitudinal problems, encounter diagnoses, yes/no questions, disability registration, and mortality data. The workbook gives every row a mapping, but some mappings need a clinical decision before they can become conformance rules.

Problem or history

A clinical assertion managed across time. Use a proposed PH problem/health-concern profile for conditions such as diabetes or hypertension.

Encounter diagnosis

A diagnosis made in the context of a visit. Use a proposed PH encounter-diagnosis profile; role and rank belong to Encounter.

Question or finding

“Does the patient have X?” and admission symptoms need a question/answer or finding model. A negative response is not an absent Condition.

Mortality assertion

An underlying cause of death needs a certified mortality model. Admission/discharge role codes do not establish cause sequence.

FHIR REST examples

These are search patterns, not a claim that the PH Core canonical host provides a FHIR REST endpoint. Replace [base] with a server that advertises Condition support in its CapabilityStatement.

GET [base]/Condition?patient=Patient/123&category=http://terminology.hl7.org/CodeSystem/condition-category|problem-list-item
GET [base]/Condition?patient=Patient/123&category=http://terminology.hl7.org/CodeSystem/condition-category|encounter-diagnosis
GET [base]/Condition?patient=Patient/123&code=http://hl7.org/fhir/sid/icd-10|E11.9

Patient, category, and code are standard R4 Condition search parameters. A new PH binding or extension is not automatically searchable; advertise supported searches explicitly.

Publication gate. Approve definitions and bindings, author FSH, compile with SUSHI, publish examples and CapabilityStatements, then validate with the exact PH Core 0.2.0 package plus FHIR R4 4.0.1. Base R4 validation alone is insufficient.
