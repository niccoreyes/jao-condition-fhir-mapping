# JAO Condition worksheet ↔ PHCDI Release 01: historical deviation study

**Purpose:** Understand how a different organization's earlier PHCDI element inventory overlaps with—or differs from—the 34 JAO Condition worksheet rows. PHCDI is **not used here as a clinically validated authority or a recommended target**. This comparison describes what the supplied CSV lists, what it omits, and where its meaning conflicts with the JAO assertion or this workspace's *separate* FHIR proposal. A generic name match is not evidence that PHCDI implemented the JAO field.

**Reading the CSV:** columns A–G describe an element ID, definition, terminology label, cardinality, type and size. Columns H–AU (`Claims Form`, surveillance CRF/CIF variants, Coronavirus Disease, HBCR/cancer forms, etc.) contain `*` for form presence. There is **no JAO column, JAO identifier, row-level JAO crosswalk, disease-registry hypertension/diabetes/COPD form column, or JAO answer/value set**. A `*` is evidence of the CSV's claimed form use of an element, **not** evidence of production exchange, a binding, or the same source field as a JAO row. Empty marks do not prove a resource is prohibited. The CSV describes a resource catalog; it does **not** show individual FHIR instances or how any JAO field was actually populated.

**Interpretation guardrails:** R4 explicitly advises that checklist questions such as “do you have a history of hypertension?” belong in [QuestionnaireResponse or Observation rather than Condition](https://hl7.org/fhir/R4/condition.html#9.2.3.5). The CSV `Condition.code` definition mentions “problem absent,” but that does not turn a missing Condition or every JAO Yes/No question into an explicit negative response. Its form stars and terminology labels do not override the JAO field's assertion type or the [Philippine policies and unresolved form options already recorded here](condition-terminology-policy-review.md).

## Open questions raised by the deviation analysis

1. **Confirm scope and authority:** Is Release 01 a form-to-resource catalog only, or a normative NHDR exchange contract? Request the dated official release, implementation guide/profile package, data dictionary, form mappings and deployed CapabilityStatement if they exist. This CSV cannot answer conformance or deployment.
2. **Resolve divergent timestamps:** CSV 228 says upload time, [base R4](https://hl7.org/fhir/R4/condition-definitions.html#Condition.recordedDate) says Condition-record creation time, and JAO 23–24 say diagnosed date/time. Define three distinct events; do not overwrite one with another. Confirm attester/time zone and paired date+time.
3. **Agree on question-to-diagnosis boundaries:** For 7, 13, 29, 37, 43 and 48, obtain source form, exact response options and status/provenance. Preserve explicit negatives and unknowns; missing Condition ≠ No. Choose Observation vs QuestionnaireResponse with owner input; create a Condition only for an independently asserted clinical diagnosis.
4. **Agree on nationally governed gaps:** PWD classification/permanence (4–5), mortality certification vs coding stage (9–10), diagnosis-recency definition (43), ICD-10-PH URI/version and registry subtype sets (25, 39, 41, 44), COPD grade/group edition (46–47), and asthma evidence/source grouping (49). Compare [policy evidence and candidate terminology](condition-terminology-policy-review.md) before approving codes; no new binding follows from this CSV.
5. **Then publish an explicit crosswalk:** For each of the **34 JAO rows**, record source assertion, PHCDI Release 01 path or documented gap, actual PH Core 0.2.0 artifact vs proposed derivative, source form/option list, required coding system/version, and QA evidence. If an exchange profile is proposed, test it against the exact published package rather than claiming it from this CSV or base R4 transaction checks.

**Observed outcome:** PHCDI lists generic `Condition`, `Observation`, `Patient`, `Encounter` and `QuestionnaireResponse` elements, but **does not document JAO-specific clinical meanings, negative-answer semantics, policy options, PWD/mortality/recency fields, or disease-registry bindings**. The current workspace's reusable-pattern proposal remains separate; no clinical endorsement of PHCDI or change to the 34-row mapping follows from this comparison.
