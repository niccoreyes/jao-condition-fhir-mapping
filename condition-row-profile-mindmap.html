How this measure is produced

Transaction and profile boundary

Thirty synthetic transaction Bundles passed the cached FHIR R4 4.0.1 JSON schema and local-reference checks. HAPI baseR4 Bundle/$validate returned no errors for representative rows 4, 17, 29, 46 and 49. Best-practice warnings and unknown PH extension information remain. This validates base R4 structure only; none of the proposed PH Core profile constraints or national terminology has been validated. Candidate SNOMED CT and LOINC concepts were checked separately on the public HL7 FHIRsmith server (tx.fhir.org/r4) at 2026-09-27 06:23 UTC; the recorded lookup returned SNOMED CT International 20250201 and LOINC 2.82. These are public terminology-membership checks, not Philippine national terminology or a binding. No transaction was committed to a server.

Rows 5, 9, 10 and 43 have no approved mapping and therefore no claimed valid transaction. Downloadable examples and validation steps are described in the mind-map notes. The independent source logical model and verbatim crosswalk remain available.
